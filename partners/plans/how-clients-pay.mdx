## What a client pays for

| | How it's billed |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscription** | The price of the client's plan, monthly or yearly, as an invoice |
| **Usage** | WhatsApp messages, AI credits and other metered products, charged to the client's wallet |

## Who funds client wallets

Open **Partner → Settings → General → How customers pay**:

| Setting | Clients | You |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Customers can add a payment method** | Add their own card, top up their wallet and pay their invoices | Can still move credit to any client |
| **You fund customer wallets** | Don't see "add a card". A card already on file is still charged | [Move credit](/partners/money/funding-clients) from your wallet. You're notified when a client runs low |

Choose **You fund customer wallets** when you bill your clients yourself and only want Karzoun to charge you.

## When a client runs low

When a client's balance drops below $20, Karzoun sends one `wallet.low_balance` event to your [partner webhook](/partners/webhook). If you fund client wallets, your support email gets a notice too.

A client that runs out can't use paid features, such as sending WhatsApp messages, until its wallet is topped up.

## Unpaid invoices

A client's open invoices show on its [detail page](/partners/clients/client-detail). You can [cover one from your wallet](/partners/money/funding-clients#cover-an-open-invoice).
