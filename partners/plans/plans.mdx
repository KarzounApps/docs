Every client workspace is on one of your plans. Open **Partner → Plans**.

## Create a plan

1. Click **Create Plan**.
2. Pick the Karzoun plan to start from. It sets which features the plan can include.
3. Set your **price**. It can't be below the system cost of the base plan; the screen shows **Your margin** for each cycle.
4. Give it a name and a description, and save.

You can have one plan per base plan type.

## Features

Click **Manage Features** to turn off features of the base plan you don't want to sell. You can't turn on features the base plan doesn't include.

**Features (English)** and **Features (Arabic)** are the bullet points shown on your signup and pricing pages, one per line, in order.

## Enterprise plans

A plan based on Karzoun's enterprise plan has **Enterprise Quotas**:

| Quota | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Free Seats**, **Max Seats** | Seats included, and the cap. `-1` = unlimited |
| **Max Channels**, **Max Storage (GB)** | Caps for the workspace |
| **Metered Product Quotas** | Free units per product (contacts, AI credits, automation runs, marketing emails, channels, WhatsApp credit line) and the rate above them |

## Show, hide or delete

- **Hide** a plan to stop offering it on signup. Clients already on it keep it.
- **Delete** removes it from your offerings. Clients already on it keep it. Some base plan types can only be hidden, not deleted.

## Per-client prices

When you [add a client](/partners/clients/add-a-client), you can give that client a custom base price, for example for a negotiated deal. The plan stays the same.
