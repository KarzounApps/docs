Meta asks businesses to verify before they can grow their WhatsApp limits. Clients can submit their documents from **Business Verification** in their workspace. Karzoun, as a Meta Business Partner, verifies them on Meta's behalf. See [Business verification](/whatsapp-api/account/business-verification).

Requests from your clients come to you first. Open **Partner → Verifications**.

## Review a request

The list shows each request with its business, workspace and status. Filter by **Pending Review** to see what's waiting.

Click **Review** to see the documents and the request's history, then:

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve** | The documents are sent to Meta. This can't be undone |
| **Return** | The client is asked to change something. Add a comment saying what |
| **Reject** | The request is closed. Add a comment with the reason |

After approval, the request moves to **Submitted to Meta**, then **Verified** or **Rejected** by Meta. The top of the page shows how many are waiting, at Meta and verified, and your success rate.

## Tips

- Check that the business name and address match the documents exactly. Mismatches are the most common reason Meta rejects.
- Return rather than reject when the client can fix the problem.
