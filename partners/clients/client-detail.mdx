---
title: "Client detail"
description: "One client's money, setup progress and users in one place."
---

Open **Partner → Organizations** and click a workspace. The page has three tabs.

## Money

- **Wallet**: a live read of spendable, metered and pending amounts. See [Balances](/partners/clients/organizations#balances).
- **Plan** and the card on file, if any.
- **Upcoming invoice**: the lines that will be on the next invoice.
- **Open invoices**: unpaid invoices, with a link to each one.
- **Usage**: usage in the current billing period, by product.
- **Lifetime**: total paid, card top-ups and paid invoices, and the first purchase.

Actions here: **Add Balance**, **Cover open invoice from wallet** and **Cancel at period end**. See [Funding clients](/partners/money/funding-clients) and [Suspend and cancel](/partners/clients/suspend-and-cancel).

## Setup

- **Activation**: which setup steps the workspace has completed.
- **Signup**: how far the owner got in the first-login wizard.
- **Source**: where the signup came from (UTM, referrer) when it was captured.

## Users

Every user of the workspace with their role and status. For each user you can:

- see their **training progress**: the lessons they've completed in the Training Center and their last activity
- **Log in as this user**. See [Log in as a user](/partners/clients/log-in-as-a-user)
