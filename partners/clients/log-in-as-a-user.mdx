---
title: "Log in as a user"
description: "Open a client's workspace as one of its users to help them or check their setup."
---

On the [client detail](/partners/clients/client-detail), open **Users** and click **Log in as this user** next to the user.

Karzoun creates a short-lived link that opens a session as that user. Everything you do is done as them, so take care with sending messages or changing settings.

Your role needs the permission to log in as client users. Ask your partner workspace owner if you don't see the option.
