---
title: "Suspend and cancel"
description: "Stop a client's access for a while, or end its subscription at the end of the period."
---

## Suspend

Suspending blocks the workspace right away: its users can't sign in and it can't send messages. Its data stays.

- In **Partner → Organizations**, select one or more clients and click **Suspend**.
- Click **Activate** to lift it.
- By API: [`partnerSuspendOrganizations`](/partners/api/reference/partner-suspend-organizations).

Use it for unpaid accounts or a client that asked to pause.

## Cancel at period end

On the [client detail](/partners/clients/client-detail), click **Cancel at period end**. The client keeps using its plan until the current period ends, then the subscription stops. Nothing is charged for the next period.

You can undo it before the period ends. Cancelling doesn't pause billing for the current period.
