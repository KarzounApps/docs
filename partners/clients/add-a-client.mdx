---
title: "Add a client"
description: "Create a client workspace, put it on a plan and give it starting credit."
---

Open **Partner → Organizations** and click **Register Organization**. To do it from your own system, use [`partnerRegisterOrganization`](/partners/api/reference/partner-register-organization).

## Fields

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin Email** | The owner. They get an invitation to set their password |
| Workspace name | Shown in the app and on invoices |
| Address | The subdomain, for example `acme` for `acme.karzoun.chat` |
| Plan | One of [your plans](/partners/plans/plans) |
| **Billing Cycle** | Monthly, or yearly if the plan has a yearly price |
| **Initial balance (USD)** | Optional. Moved from your wallet right after the workspace is created. It can't be more than your available balance |

### Advanced

| Field | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Custom Base Price ($)** | A negotiated price for this client. Leave empty for the plan price |
| **Defer First Invoice (days)** | Delay the first charge by up to 30 days, for example when the client already paid you. Empty uses the plan's default |
| **Original Start Date** | For clients moving from another system who already paid. The subscription is backdated (up to one year) so the next renewal lines up |

## What happens next

- The workspace is created on your brand and appears in your list.
- The owner receives the invitation.
- `client.created` goes to your [partner webhook](/partners/webhook).
- If you set an initial balance, it's in the client's wallet. If that transfer fails, the workspace is still created and you can [add balance](/partners/money/funding-clients) later.

Next, the client connects WhatsApp in their workspace or from a [connect link](/partners/connect-links).
