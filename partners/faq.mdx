---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Common questions about the Karzoun partner program."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I sell both the platform and the WhatsApp API?">
    Yes. Each client is on one of your plans. Give platform clients a plan based on a platform plan, and API-only clients a plan based on the API plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do my clients see Karzoun?">
    Not if your account has custom branding. Your logo, colors, login page and email look apply to every client workspace, and you can hide "Powered by". Client workspace addresses end in `karzoun.chat`. See [Branding](/partners/brand/branding).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I set my own prices?">
    Yes, at or above the system cost of the base plan. You can also give one client a custom price when you add it. See [Plans](/partners/plans/plans).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who pays for WhatsApp messages?">
    Each client's wallet. Either the client tops it up by card, or you move credit to it from your wallet. See [How clients pay](/partners/plans/how-clients-pay).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I bill my clients myself?">
    Yes. Set **How customers pay** to **You fund customer wallets**, invoice your clients on your side, and fund their wallets from yours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get paid?">
    You earn commission on your clients' paid subscription invoices, under your partner agreement. See [Earnings](/partners/money/earnings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a client connect WhatsApp without logging in to Karzoun?">
    Yes, with a [connect link](/partners/connect-links) you sign on your server.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see inside a client's workspace?">
    Yes. [Log in as a user](/partners/clients/log-in-as-a-user) of that workspace. Use it to help, and take care: actions are done as that user.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I move an existing client to Karzoun?">
    Yes. When you add the client, set **Original Start Date** so the renewal lines up with what they already paid, and **Defer First Invoice** if needed. Their WhatsApp number can join from another provider; see [Numbers already on WhatsApp](/partners/connect-links#numbers-already-on-whatsapp).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
