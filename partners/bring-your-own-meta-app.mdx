title: "Bring your own Meta app"
description: "Onboard clients with your own Meta app through a Multi-Partner Solution with Karzoun."
---

If you are a Meta **Tech Provider** and want your own Meta app to access your clients' WhatsApp accounts, you can run Embedded Signup with your app through a **Multi-Partner Solution** with Karzoun. Your app and Karzoun's app then share each client's WhatsApp Business Account, and messaging is still billed through Karzoun.

Most partners don't need this. [Connect links](/partners/connect-links) already run signup on your own branded domain.

## When it's worth it

- You build on Meta's APIs directly as well as on Karzoun.
- You want clients' assets visible in your own Business Manager.
- You want to own the signup UI end to end.

## What you need

- A verified business and a Meta app approved for `whatsapp_business_management` and `whatsapp_business_messaging`, with advanced access to `public_profile`.
- A Multi-Partner Solution created in your app with Karzoun's app as Solution Partner.
- Embedded Signup hosted on your domain with your app's Facebook Login for Business configuration.

## Availability

Available on request. Contact the Karzoun partnerships team and we'll set it up with you.
