The Karzoun partner program lets you run your own customer business on top of Karzoun. You create a workspace for each client, under your brand and your prices, and manage all of them from one partner portal.

## Who it's for

- **Agencies** that set up and run WhatsApp, inbox and automation for their clients.
- **Resellers** that sell Karzoun plans in their market, at their own prices.
- **Software platforms** (e-commerce, CRM, booking, ERP) that give their users WhatsApp through the [WhatsApp API](/whatsapp-api).

## What you can sell

| | Your clients get |
| --- | --- |
| **The Karzoun platform** | Shared inbox, WhatsApp and other channels, automation, campaigns, AI, contacts and reports, under your brand |
| **The WhatsApp API** | API keys and webhooks for their numbers, to connect WhatsApp to their own software or yours |
| **Both** | Any mix, one client at a time. Each client is on the plan you choose |
