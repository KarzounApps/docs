## Your partner workspace

Your partner account is a Karzoun workspace with an extra area: the **Partner portal**. Open it from your workspace at `/partner`. Everything in these docs happens there unless a page says otherwise.

The portal has these screens:

| Screen | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Earnings, clients that need attention, quick actions |
| **Organizations** | All your clients: balances, usage, setup progress. Add and manage clients |
| **Plans** | The plans you sell and their prices |
| **Earnings** | Your wallet, commissions and payouts |
| **Reports** | Signups and revenue over time |
| **Verifications** | Your clients' business verification requests |
| **Settings** | Branding, domains, email, WhatsApp verification, signup and developer settings |

## Client workspaces

Each client is its own Karzoun workspace (an **organization**) linked to your partner account. It has:

- its own address, like `acme.karzoun.chat`, showing your brand
- its own users, channels, contacts and data. Clients never see each other
- a plan, from [your plans](/partners/plans/plans)
- a wallet that pays for its usage, such as WhatsApp messages and AI

A client workspace can be created by you (in the portal or [by API](/partners/api/reference/partner-register-organization)), or by the client through [self-signup](/partners/plans/self-signup) on your domain.

## Money

Two things are billed to a client:

- **The subscription** for its plan, at the price you set. Billed monthly or yearly.
- **Usage** from its wallet, such as WhatsApp messages and AI credits.

[How clients pay](/partners/plans/how-clients-pay) decides whether clients add their own card or you fund their wallets. Either way, you can [move credit](/partners/money/funding-clients) from your own wallet to a client.

You earn [commission](/partners/money/earnings) on your clients' paid subscription invoices.

## Your brand

Your [branding](/partners/brand/branding) applies to your partner workspace and every client workspace: logo, colors, login page, emails. A client on a plan with custom branding can add its own brand on top of yours.

## Karzoun's role

Karzoun runs the platform, connects and registers WhatsApp numbers as the Meta partner, and bills usage. You own the client relationship: sales, onboarding, first-line support and your own invoices to your clients, if you bill them separately.
