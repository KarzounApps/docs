---
title: "Email"
description: "Send invitations, password resets and notices to your clients from your own email address."
---

By default, emails to your clients' users come from Karzoun's address with your [branding](/partners/brand/branding). To send them from your own domain, connect your SMTP server.

Open **Partner → Settings → Email**.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **SMTP Host**, **Port**, **Username**, **Password** | From your email provider |
| **Use SSL/TLS** | On for port 465. Off for STARTTLS on port 587 |
| **Sender Name**, **Sender Email** | What recipients see. The sender email only applies once an SMTP server is set |

Click **Test Connection** to check that Karzoun can reach your server. The password is stored encrypted and never shown again; enter a new value to replace it.

<Tip>
  Set up SPF and DKIM for your sending domain at your email provider, so emails don't land in spam.
</Tip>
