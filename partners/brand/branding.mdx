title: "Branding"
description: "Your logo, colors, login page and email look, on your portal and every client workspace."
---

Open **Partner → Settings → Branding**. Changes show in the live preview as you type and apply after **Save Changes**.

<Note>
  Branding is opened per partner account. If you can't save, ask your account manager to enable custom branding.
</Note>

## Where your brand shows

- the app, for your users and every client workspace
- the login, signup and password pages
- emails sent to your clients' users
- the browser tab

## Settings

| Section | Settings |
| --- | --- |
| **Identity** | Logo (sidebar and login page), small logo (collapsed sidebar), favicon |
| **Colors** | Primary, text on primary, background, text, sidebar background and text, accent. A badge shows whether text is readable (WCAG) |
| **Typography & shape** | Font, corner radius, default theme (light, dark or match system) for users who haven't chosen one |
| **Login page** | Page title, subtitle and background image |
| **Email** | Email logo and footer text |
| **Advanced** | Show or hide "Powered by", and custom CSS (up to 50 KB) |

**Generate from primary** fills the other colors from your primary color. **Reset to Defaults** goes back to the default brand. A field you leave empty uses the default.

## Clients with their own brand

A client on a plan that includes custom branding can set its own logo and colors in its workspace. Its brand applies to its own workspace and sits on top of yours. Fields it doesn't set keep your values.
