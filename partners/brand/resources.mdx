---
title: "Resources and links"
description: "Help, legal, social and app links shown to your clients."
---

Open **Partner → Settings → General → Resources & Legal**. These links appear on the signup page and inside client workspaces while your brand is active.

| Link | Where it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Help Center URL**, **Documentation URL** | Help menu |
| **Terms of Service URL**, **Privacy Policy URL** | Signup page and footer |
| **Facebook**, **LinkedIn**, **Instagram**, **YouTube**, **Video tutorials** | Help menu and inbox |
| **iOS app**, **Android app** | Help menu |

A link you leave empty is hidden. Your clients never see Karzoun's own links in its place.
