---
title: "WhatsApp verification at signup"
description: "Verify new clients' phone numbers with a WhatsApp code sent from your own number."
---

When [self-signup](/partners/plans/self-signup) is on, you can ask new clients to confirm their phone number with a one-time code on WhatsApp, sent from your own number.

## Set it up

1. In your partner workspace, connect a WhatsApp number and get an **authentication** template approved with a code. See [Authentication templates](/whatsapp-api/templates/authentication).
2. Open **Partner → Settings → WhatsApp**.
3. Choose the **WhatsApp channel**, then the **OTP Template**.
4. Save.

The codes are sent from your number and billed to your workspace wallet like any other template message.
