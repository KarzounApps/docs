You have two balances in Karzoun:

| | What it is | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workspace wallet** | Credit you've added to your partner workspace. You move it to clients and it pays your own usage | **Billing** in your partner workspace |
| **Earnings** | Commission you've earned on clients' invoices | **Partner → Earnings** |

## Workspace wallet

Top it up by card from **Billing → Wallet** in your partner workspace, like any Karzoun workspace. It funds:

- [transfers to clients](/partners/money/funding-clients), including the initial balance when you add a client
- [covering a client's open invoice](/partners/money/funding-clients#cover-an-open-invoice)
- your own workspace's usage, such as WhatsApp messages from your own numbers

Its available balance shows on the Partner overview and when you add balance to a client. By API: `wallet` on the [`partner`](/partners/api/reference/partner) query.
