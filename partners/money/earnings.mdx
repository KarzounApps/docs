Open **Partner → Earnings**.

## How you earn

You earn commission when your clients pay their subscription invoices. The rates are set in your partner agreement. Each paid invoice creates a commission entry.

On the [Plans](/partners/plans/plans) page, **Your margin** shows the difference between your price and the system cost of each plan.

## What you see

| | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Earned**, **Pending Amount**, **Paid Amount** | Your commission so far, what's not paid out yet and what was |
| **Commission Summary** | Lifetime totals, average commission and monthly growth |
| **Revenue Forecast** | Projected commission for the next 3 months, from upcoming renewals |
| **Recent commissions** | The latest entries. **View all** opens the full list |

## Commissions list

Each entry shows the client, plan, billing period, base amount, rate and commission. Filter by status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Earned, not paid out yet |
| **Processing** | In a payout being prepared |
| **Paid** | Paid out to you |
| **Reversed** | Taken back, for example after a refund |
| **Cancelled** | Not payable |

Click an entry for its line items.

## Payouts

Payouts of your earnings follow your partner agreement. Contact your account manager to arrange one.
