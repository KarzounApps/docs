---
title: "Reports"
description: "Signups and revenue across your clients over time."
---

Open **Partner → Reports**. Pick a range (last 7, 30 or 90 days, 6 months or a year) and daily, weekly or monthly steps.

| Report | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Registrations** | New client workspaces per period, with how many are active and suspended |
| **Revenue** | Subscription and one-time revenue from your clients per period, with growth against the previous period |

For what you earned from that revenue, see [Earnings](/partners/money/earnings).
