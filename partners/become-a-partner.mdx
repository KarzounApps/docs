## Apply

Contact the Karzoun partnerships team through [karzoun.chat/contact](https://karzoun.chat/contact). Tell us:

- who your clients are and how many you expect in the first months
- what you'll sell: the Karzoun platform, the WhatsApp API, or both
- whether you need your own domain and brand

## What we set up

After approval, Karzoun turns your workspace into a partner workspace. You then see the **Partner portal** at `/partner` in your workspace.

Some features are opened per partner account. Ask your account manager for any you need:

| Feature | Without it |
| --- | --- |
| Custom branding | Your clients see the default brand |
| Custom domains | Clients use `*.karzoun.chat` addresses |
| [Your own Meta app](/partners/bring-your-own-meta-app) | Clients connect WhatsApp through Karzoun's Meta app, which works for everyone |
