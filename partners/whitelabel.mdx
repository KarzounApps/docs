Partners can offer Karzoun under their own brand.

## Custom domain

- UI: `https://{tenant}.yourdomain.com`
- API: `https://{tenant}.api.yourdomain.com/graphql`

Configure DNS and domain verification in **Partner Settings**.

## Branding

Upload logo, colors, and support links. Tenant users see partner branding while running on Karzoun infrastructure.
