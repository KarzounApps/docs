**Karzoun Chat** is the conversation and growth operating system for businesses in Saudi Arabia and the region. It brings customer channels — led by official WhatsApp Business — together with your team, automations, campaigns, Karzoun Pixel, AI, and messaging commerce in one workspace that non-technical teams can run, and that scales from a small shop to an enterprise.

One place to operate customer conversations — platform power, team-friendly clarity.

## What you can do with Karzoun

Six systems that share one customer record, one inbox, and the same context — deep enough to stand alone, stronger together.

| Focus | What it means for your team |
| --- | --- |
| **Unified inbox** | WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Messenger, email, and webchat in one queue — with customer context beside every conversation |
| **WhatsApp Business** | Official API, templates, flows, commerce in the chat, and calling from the conversation |
| **Automation** | Visual no-code paths: welcome, follow-up, assign, recover carts, sync with your store |
| **Growth** | Karzoun Pixel, segments, and campaigns on WhatsApp, email, and push — reach the right person |
| **AI** | Copilot for your team and agents for customers — with knowledge, skills, and clear escalation |
| **Team workspace** | Tasks, internal chat, meetings, attendance, roles, and reporting as you scale |
