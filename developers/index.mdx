Welcome to Karzoun developer documentation — quickstarts, schema reference, webhooks, MCP, and SDK guides for anyone building on the platform.

<Info>
  **Who is this for?** Partner app developers (Salla, Zid, embedded commerce), integration engineers, and teams wiring AI agents that need live access to customer data.
</Info>

Sales, support, and marketing run on one workspace in Karzoun — your integration inherits that unity. One app token, one permission model, one customer record across channels.

Use **GraphQL** when you query or mutate on demand, **webhooks** when your service reacts to events, and **MCP** when an AI client needs tool access.

## What Karzoun powers

| Module | What you can reach |
| --- | --- |
| Shared inbox | Omnichannel conversations — WhatsApp, email, webchat, Messenger, Instagram |
| CRM & segments | Customers, companies, tags, custom fields, and dynamic segments |
| Automations | Triggers, conditions, and actions across inbox and channels |
| AI agents | Copilot and agents grounded in CRM and conversation context |
| Marketing | WhatsApp and email broadcasts, templates, and analytics |
| Operations | Tasks, orders, meetings, files, and team chat |

<Note>
  Field descriptions live in the [GraphQL reference](/api-reference) (synced from the gateway). Start with [Quickstart](/developers/getting-started/quickstart), then browse the schema or test queries in the [Playground](https://karzoun.chat/developer/playground).
</Note>
