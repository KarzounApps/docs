---
title: "Coexistence"
description: "Use the WhatsApp Business app and the WhatsApp API on the same number."
---

Coexistence connects a number that's already on the **WhatsApp Business app** to the WhatsApp API, without losing the app, the number or its chat history. Send at scale through the API and keep chatting one-to-one in the app.

## Connect a number with coexistence

When you [connect a number](/whatsapp-api/dashboard/connect-a-number), choose **Use my WhatsApp Business app**. Meta asks you to confirm the number, then shows a QR code or an access code: open the WhatsApp Business app on your phone, tap the message from WhatsApp and scan the code.

You can also sync your chat history and contacts from the app. Meta allows one sync per connection, within 24 hours of connecting, and sends it to your webhook.

## How messages sync

- Messages you send through the API appear in the app automatically.
- Messages you send from the app reach your webhook as `smb_message_echoes` events, so your system stays in sync.
- Chat history and contacts shared at connection arrive as `history` and `smb_app_state_sync` events.

## Pricing

Messages you send from the app stay free. Messages sent through the API are billed like any API message, and you still need a template to start a conversation through the API.

## Keep it connected

- Open the WhatsApp Business app at least once every **13 days**. Otherwise the connection drops.
- Don't uninstall the app.
- After connecting, linked devices are unlinked. Re-link only supported ones: WhatsApp for Windows and Wear OS aren't supported, and messages sent from unsupported devices don't reach your webhook.

## Limitations

| Not available on coexistence numbers | Alternative |
| --- | --- |
| Official Business Account (blue badge) | Meta Verified for Business |
| Automatic display name review | Subscribe to Meta Verified to start it |
| Calling API | Use a Cloud API number |
| Disappearing, view-once and live location messages | — |
| Broadcast lists in the app | They become read-only |

If you can't open the app every 13 days, connect the number as a regular Cloud API number instead.
