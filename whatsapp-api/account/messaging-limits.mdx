---
title: "Messaging limits"
description: "How many people you can start conversations with per day, and how the limit grows."
---

Your messaging limit is the number of **unique people** you can send business-initiated messages to in a rolling 24-hour period. Replies inside an open [customer service window](/whatsapp-api/customer-service-window) don't count.

The limit applies to your whole **business portfolio** and is shared by all its numbers. One busy number can use up the limit for the others.

## Tiers

| Limit | How you get there |
| --- | --- |
| **250** | Where every new business starts |
| **2,000** | Complete one scaling path (below) |
| **10,000** | Automatic |
| **100,000** | Automatic |
| **Unlimited** | Automatic |

Only Meta raises limits. Neither Karzoun nor any other provider can request an increase.

## Raise your limit

### To 2,000: scaling paths

Complete **one** of these:

- [Verify your business](/whatsapp-api/account/business-verification), with Karzoun's partner-led verification or in Meta Business Suite.
- Send **2,000 delivered** business-initiated messages to unique users within a rolling 30 days, using high-quality templates.

Meta then reviews your message quality:

| Approved | Denied |
| --- | --- |
| The limit rises to 2,000 at once | The limit stays the same |
| Email and developer alert | Email with next steps: verify, improve quality, or send more qualifying messages |
| `business_capability_update` webhook event | |

### Beyond 2,000: automatic scaling

From 2,000 on, Meta checks your account continuously. You move to the next tier, usually within 6 hours, when:

- your numbers and templates have high [quality](/whatsapp-api/account/quality-rating), and
- you used at least **half** of your current limit in the last 7 days.

## What happens at the limit

Messages to new people beyond your limit fail until the 24-hour window moves on. Replies within open conversations still go through.

<Tip>
  Planning a campaign? Count unique recipients, not messages. With a 2,000 limit, a 5,000-person send needs three days or a higher tier. See the [campaign checklist](/whatsapp-api/guides/campaign-checklist).
</Tip>

## Check your limit

- The number's **Profile** tab in your workspace (messaging limit).
- `messagingLimitTier` in the Partner API's [`partnerChannels`](/partners/api/numbers-and-keys).
- **Meta Business Suite → WhatsApp Manager → Account tools → Messaging limits**.

## Meta-provided +1 555 numbers

A free US number from Meta can't send messages until your portfolio is verified or already has a limit of 2,000 or more.
