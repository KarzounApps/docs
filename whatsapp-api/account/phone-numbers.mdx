## Requirements

A number you connect must:

- belong to your business,
- include a country code and area code (short codes aren't supported),
- receive an SMS or a voice call for verification, unless it's already on the WhatsApp Business app and you connect it with [coexistence](/whatsapp-api/account/coexistence).

Landlines and virtual numbers work if they can receive the verification call. Karzoun doesn't sell phone numbers; use one your business already owns.

### Numbers already on WhatsApp

| The number is on | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing | Connect it directly |
| The WhatsApp Business app | Connect it with **Use my WhatsApp Business app** to keep the app ([coexistence](/whatsapp-api/account/coexistence)) |
| Personal WhatsApp | Delete the WhatsApp account in the app first (**Settings → Account → Delete account**), then connect it |
| Another WhatsApp API provider | Add Karzoun to it. See [Use a number from another provider](/whatsapp-api/account/existing-numbers) |

A number banned on WhatsApp must be unbanned through WhatsApp's appeal before you can connect it.

Once connected to the API, the number still makes normal calls and SMS, but it can't be used in the personal WhatsApp app.

## How many numbers

A new business portfolio can register **2** numbers. Meta raises the cap up to **20** automatically when the business is verified, or when a number reaches a 2,000 messaging limit and sends enough paid messages. You get a `business_capability_update` webhook event and a Meta Business Suite notification when it changes.

Verified for more than a week and still at 2? Improve your numbers' [quality](/whatsapp-api/account/quality-rating); Meta re-evaluates the cap automatically.

Each number gets its own [API key](/whatsapp-api/authentication) and [webhook](/whatsapp-api/webhooks/overview).

## Phone number status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `CONNECTED` | Registered and able to send |
| `PENDING` | Registration not finished. Finish connecting the number |
| `FLAGGED` | Quality dropped to low. Fix it within 7 days to avoid a lower limit |
| `RESTRICTED` | Messaging limit reached, or limited by a policy action |
| `BANNED` / `DISCONNECTED` | Can't send. See [Account status](/whatsapp-api/account/account-status) |

Check it any time with [`GET /health_status`](/whatsapp-api/messaging/number-settings#health-status) or on the number's **Profile** tab.

## Two-step verification

Two-step verification adds a 6-digit PIN to the number. With Meta's current account model you don't need to turn it off to connect a number or add a provider. One case still needs it: the [business account locked](/whatsapp-api/account/account-status#business-account-locked) error.

To turn it off: **Meta Business Suite → WhatsApp Manager → Phone numbers →** the number **→ Settings → Two-step verification → Turn off**. Meta emails a confirmation link to the portfolio's admins.

## Remove a number

Deleting a number from the WhatsApp Business Platform frees it for the WhatsApp app or another account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check you can">
    You need admin access to the business portfolio, and the number must not have sent paid messages in the last 30 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete it in WhatsApp Manager">
    **Meta Business Suite → WhatsApp Manager → Phone numbers**, click the trash icon next to the number, pick a reason and confirm with your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove it from Karzoun">
    Remove the number in your workspace so its API key and webhook stop working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

There's no API to delete a number; it's only possible in WhatsApp Manager.
