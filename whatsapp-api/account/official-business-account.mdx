An Official Business Account (OBA) shows a blue badge next to your display name. Meta grants it to authentic, notable brands. It's issued per phone number and display name.

## Who qualifies

- Your business follows the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy.
- The number has been on the WhatsApp Business Platform for at least 30 days.
- Your business portfolio is [verified](/whatsapp-api/account/business-verification).
- The number has two-step verification on and an approved [display name](/whatsapp-api/account/display-names).
- The number isn't a [coexistence](/whatsapp-api/account/coexistence) number. Those can use Meta Verified for Business instead.
- The brand is **notable**: well known and often searched for.

## Notability

Meta judges notability from coverage in news publications with sizable audiences. Paid or promotional content, business listings, app store pages and your own social media don't count.

Make your application stronger:

- Your website clearly describes what you do and shows the same display name, address and phone number.
- Include up to 5 links to articles about your brand from reputable publications, all from the last 12 months.
- Leave out sponsored articles, your own site and social profiles.
- Add context Meta can use: countries of operation, parent brand, primary language.

## Apply

1. Open **Meta Business Suite → WhatsApp Manager → Phone numbers** and select the number.
2. On the **Profile** tab, find **Official business account** and click **Submit request**.
3. Fill in the form with your supporting links.

If **Submit request** is greyed out while you meet the criteria, contact Karzoun support and we'll review your case with Meta.

## After a decision

- **Approved:** the badge appears next to your display name.
- **Denied:** decisions can't be appealed. Build more coverage and apply again after 30 days. Your business profile (photo, description, website) stays fully editable meanwhile.

OBA is free. Only Meta decides, so no provider can guarantee approval.
