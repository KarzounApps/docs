---
title: "Use a number from another provider"
description: "Add Karzoun to a WhatsApp number that already works with another provider, without losing it."
---

WhatsApp numbers can work with more than one provider. Under Meta's account model, a number lives in a **WhatsApp account**, and each provider gets its own **Messaging account** for templates, billing and webhooks. Adding Karzoun doesn't remove your current provider, and you don't need to turn off two-step verification or re-register the number.

## Add Karzoun

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    In your workspace, click **Add a number**. An admin of your Meta Business Portfolio must do this; nobody can complete it on your behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the existing number">
    On Meta's first screen, pick the number you already use. The portfolio and WhatsApp account fields are greyed out because Meta pre-selects the ones that hold the number. That's expected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish signup">
    Karzoun creates its Messaging account on the number and starts billing it to your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route conversations to Karzoun">
    Required. See below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Route conversations to Karzoun

When a number has several providers, Meta decides which one handles conversations, and it keeps the previous provider until you change it. Until you do:

- incoming messages and status webhooks go to the other provider, not to you,
- replies fail with error `131070` ("another app is controlling this thread"),
- `health_status` may say the app isn't subscribed to message webhooks.

Fix it in Meta Business Suite: **Settings → WhatsApp accounts →** the account **→** the number **→ Conversation routing → Manage**, choose **Karzoun** in every dropdown and click **Apply**. Only a portfolio admin can do this. Removing the previous provider from the number also resets routing.

## Templates

Templates belong to a Messaging account, so they don't move with the number. Create the templates you need again in Karzoun, from the dashboard or the [API](/whatsapp-api/templates/manage).

## Limits and quality

Messaging limits and throughput are per number and portfolio, so they're shared across providers on the same number. Your [quality rating](/whatsapp-api/account/quality-rating) also carries over.

## Remove the previous provider (optional)

In Meta Business Suite, open the WhatsApp account's **Partners** settings and remove the old provider. Do it after conversation routing points to Karzoun and you've confirmed incoming messages arrive.

## Numbers on the WhatsApp apps

- **WhatsApp Business app:** connect with [coexistence](/whatsapp-api/account/coexistence) to keep the app.
- **Personal WhatsApp:** delete the WhatsApp account in the app, then connect the number.
