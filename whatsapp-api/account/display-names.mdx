---
title: "Display names"
description: "The business name customers see on WhatsApp: rules, approval and changes."
---

The display name is the business name WhatsApp shows in chats and notifications. Until it's approved and visible, customers see your phone number instead.

## When it becomes visible

All three must be true:

1. The display name is **approved** (check the number's **Profile** tab or WhatsApp Manager).
2. Your business is [verified](/whatsapp-api/account/business-verification).
3. You've reached the 2,000 [messaging limit](/whatsapp-api/account/messaging-limits), or sent 2,000 delivered business-initiated messages to unique users within 30 days.

Then the name appears automatically.

<Note>
  [Coexistence](/whatsapp-api/account/coexistence) numbers aren't reviewed automatically. Subscribe to Meta Verified for Business to start the review.
</Note>

## Rules

- It must clearly relate to your business, and match how your brand appears on your website, including capitals and spacing. `Acme Store`, `ACME store` and `AcmeStore` count as different names.
- It must follow WhatsApp's Commerce and Business policies: no generic words alone ("Shop", "Pizza"), no personal names unless that's the brand, no misleading claims.
- It should match or clearly relate to the legal name in your business portfolio.

## Change the display name

Change it on the number's **Profile** tab in your workspace, or in WhatsApp Manager. Before registration you can change it freely; after that, wait **30 days** between change requests.

<Warning>
  Changing the name of an [Official Business Account](/whatsapp-api/account/official-business-account) sends it back to review, and the badge isn't guaranteed to stay.
</Warning>

## If it's rejected

First check two things:

- **Legal name:** the legal name in your business portfolio relates clearly to the display name.
- **Website:** the display name, or a clear reference to it, appears on your website.

Common reasons for rejection: no clear link between the legal entity and the name, the name isn't on the website, or branding differs across your assets.

To appeal, resubmit the same or an updated name in WhatsApp Manager and explain the link when Meta asks. The result arrives on your webhook as `phone_number_name_update`.
