Your WhatsApp Business Account has a status that decides what you can send. It applies to every number in the account.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Approved** | No restrictions |
| **Review in progress** | Meta is reviewing the account. You can't message until it's approved |
| **Not approved** | The account doesn't meet policy. Fix the cause and it can be approved |
| **Disabled** | Disabled for policy violations. Appeal to restore it |

See it in Meta Business Suite on the WhatsApp account. Changes arrive on your [webhook](/whatsapp-api/webhooks/events) as `account_update`.

## Review in progress

New accounts are reviewed against the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy. Most reviews finish within hours. Speed it up by making sure:

- Business Info in your portfolio is complete (legal name, address, website, phone).
- Your website is live on `https` and clearly describes what you sell or do.
- Nothing you sell is on WhatsApp's prohibited list.

## Policy enforcement

When Meta finds a policy violation, enforcement escalates:

1. **Warning** about the violated policy.
2. **1 or 3-day block** on business-initiated messages and on adding numbers.
3. **5 or 7-day block** on all messages, including replies, and on adding numbers.
4. **Permanent disable** if violations continue.

Severe violations (for example child exploitation, terrorism, illegal drugs) disable the account immediately. High-risk categories such as adult content, alcohol, tobacco, gambling and unsafe supplements escalate faster.

### Where warnings arrive

- `account_update` webhook events, with the restriction type and duration.
- WhatsApp Manager and the Meta Business Suite notification center.
- Email to the portfolio's admins ("Your WhatsApp Business account is violating policy").

Act on the first warning. Ignored warnings lead to longer blocks.

## Appeal

You can appeal any violation within **90 days**, in **Meta Business Suite → Business Support Home**. Explain what you changed.

## Prevent violations

- [ ] Your catalog contains no prohibited items and complete product information.
- [ ] Your website is live on `https` and shows the same address and phone number as your portfolio.
- [ ] Your templates follow the policy.
- [ ] Your portfolio and WhatsApp profile describe the business clearly.
- [ ] You watch `account_update` events and react the same day.

## Business account locked

Sends can fail with "Business account has been locked", often after an account was restricted and then reinstated.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn off two-step verification">
    See [Two-step verification](/whatsapp-api/account/phone-numbers#two-step-verification).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconnect the number">
    Connect the number again in your workspace. In Meta's signup, select the **existing** WhatsApp account and the **same** number. Don't create new ones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn two-step verification back on">
    Then check [`GET /health_status`](/whatsapp-api/messaging/number-settings#health-status) shows the number as available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Karzoun's access to your account

Karzoun needs full control of your WhatsApp account as your provider. If someone removes Karzoun as a partner in Meta Business Suite, the number stops sending. To restore it, reconnect the number in your workspace and select the existing account.
