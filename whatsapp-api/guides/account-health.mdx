title: "Keep your account healthy"

A healthy account sends without surprises: templates stay approved, limits grow and nothing gets blocked. These are the signals to watch and what to do about each.

## What to watch

| Signal | Where | Act when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Number quality | **Profile** tab, `phone_number_quality_update` | It turns yellow or red |
| Template quality | **Templates** tab, `message_template_quality_update` | A template turns yellow or red |
| Template status | `message_template_status_update` | A template is paused or rejected |
| Account status | `account_update` | Any warning or restriction |
| Messaging limit | **Profile** tab, `business_capability_update` | You're close to it before a campaign |
| Webhook health | **API** tab, **View deliveries** | Events are retrying or failing |
| Wallet | **Billing** | The balance won't cover the next few days |

Route these webhook events to an alert channel your team reads, not only to logs.

## When quality drops

1. Find the cause. Check which templates were sent most in the last 7 days and their read rates on the **Insights** tab.
2. Pause or fix the weak templates. Make the sender and value obvious in the first line.
3. Narrow the audience to people who engaged recently.
4. Slow down. Fewer, better-targeted messages restore quality faster than anything else.

See [Quality rating](/whatsapp-api/account/quality-rating) and [Template quality](/whatsapp-api/templates/overview#template-quality).

## When you get a policy warning

Act the same day. Read the violated policy in the `account_update` event or Meta's email, remove the content that caused it, and appeal within 90 days if you think it's wrong. See [Account status](/whatsapp-api/account/account-status).

## Habits that keep accounts healthy

- Message only people who [opted in](/whatsapp-api/opt-in), and respect opt-outs at once.
- Prefer utility over marketing where it fits, and keep marketing relevant.
- Keep templates few and good. Delete ones you don't use.
- Answer users quickly inside the [customer service window](/whatsapp-api/customer-service-window).
- Keep your business profile, website and portfolio details accurate.
- Verify your business. It raises your limit and makes reviews smoother.
