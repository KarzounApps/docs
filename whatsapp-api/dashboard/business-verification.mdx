---

Open **Verification** at the top of your dashboard. It lists the Meta businesses you connected to Karzoun, with the verification status of each.

Verifying your business raises your [messaging limit](/whatsapp-api/account/messaging-limits), lets your [display name](/whatsapp-api/account/display-names) show in chats, lets you register more [phone numbers](/whatsapp-api/account/phone-numbers) and apply for an [Official Business Account](/whatsapp-api/account/official-business-account). See [Business verification](/whatsapp-api/account/business-verification) for what Meta checks.

## Verify a business

1. Click **Start Verification** on the business.
2. **Confirm** the business. You can add a **Note for Reviewer**.
3. Fill in the **Details**: business vertical, website, phone and email, matching your documents.
4. Upload your **Documents**: a commercial registration or freelance certificate (required), plus up to 2 supporting files such as a tax certificate or proof of address. PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB each. The legal name and address must match the business.
5. Choose a **Service Tier**: **Normal** (24–72 hours), **Express** (1–3 hours) or **Urgent** (5–10 minutes). Karzoun reviews every request before it goes to Meta. The price shows on each tier and is charged to your wallet.
6. Submit.

To verify for free instead, apply yourself in Meta Business Suite. It usually takes longer. See [Ways to verify](/whatsapp-api/account/business-verification#ways-to-verify).

## After you submit

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending Review** | Waiting for Karzoun's review before it goes to Meta |
| **Returned** | Something needs changing. Read the note, then click **Edit & Resubmit** |
| **At Meta** | Sent to Meta. Meta usually decides within a few business days |
| **Verified** | Done. Your new limits and features apply to every number under this business |
| **Rejected** | Meta or the reviewer declined it. Read the reason, then click **Submit New Request** |

Once verified, apply for the blue badge in Meta's WhatsApp Manager. See [Official Business Account](/whatsapp-api/account/official-business-account).

## A business isn't listed

The list shows the businesses that own the numbers you connected, and businesses you gave Karzoun access to. To add another Meta business, click **Connect Business** (or **Connect Business Account** when the list is empty). It opens a Facebook sign-in in a new tab; come back and refresh when you're done.

Only a business connected to your workspace can be verified.
