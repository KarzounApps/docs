---

The **Insights** tab shows what happened to the messages a number sent, using Meta's analytics.

## What you can see

| Chart | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Message delivery funnel | Sent → delivered → read → clicked |
| Volume over time | Messages per day or week |
| Cost over time | What the messages cost |
| Cost per delivered message, cost per URL click | Efficiency of your sends |
| Clicks by button | Which template buttons people tap |
| Breakdown | The same metrics by country, category or template |

Pick a date range at the top. Data from Meta can take a few hours to appear.

## Use it to

- **Spot quality problems early.** A template with a falling read rate is often one people don't want. Fix it before it's [paused](/whatsapp-api/templates/overview#pausing).
- **Compare templates.** Select templates in the **Templates** tab and click **Insights**.
- **Plan budgets.** Cost per country and category shows where your spend goes.

To pull template metrics into your own reports, use the [template analytics API](/whatsapp-api/templates/analytics). For what you're charged from your wallet, see [Billing](/whatsapp-api/dashboard/billing).
