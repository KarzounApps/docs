---
title: "Connect a number"
description: "Connect a WhatsApp number to Karzoun with Meta's signup: new numbers, WhatsApp Business app numbers and numbers from another provider."
---

You connect numbers from your workspace through Meta's signup window. It creates or picks your Meta business portfolio, WhatsApp Business Account and number, and gives Karzoun access to them.

## Before you start

- **Meta admin access.** Log in with a Facebook account that's an admin of your business portfolio, or can create one.
- **A phone number** that can receive an SMS or voice call for verification, or a number already on the WhatsApp Business app.
- **A live website** on `https` that describes your business. Meta reviews it.
- **A display name** that matches your business. See [Display names](/whatsapp-api/account/display-names).

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the signup">
    In your workspace, open **WhatsApp** and click **Add a number** (or **Connect number** during onboarding). Allow pop-ups if your browser blocks the Meta window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to Meta">
    Log in with your Facebook account and accept the permissions Karzoun requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your business">
    Pick your business portfolio or create one. Fill in your business name, website and country as they appear on official documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a WhatsApp account">
    Pick an existing WhatsApp Business Account or create a new one. Choose the display name and category shown to customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the number">
    Enter the number and verify it with the code Meta sends by SMS or voice call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Back in Karzoun, the number appears in your workspace. Create an [API key](/whatsapp-api/dashboard/api-tab) and start sending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which option to pick

| Your number today | What to choose |
| --- | --- |
| Not on WhatsApp | Add it as a new number |
| On the **WhatsApp Business app**, and you want to keep using the app | **Use my WhatsApp Business app** ([coexistence](/whatsapp-api/account/coexistence)) |
| On the WhatsApp Business app, and you don't need the app anymore | Delete the WhatsApp account in the app, then add it as a new number |
| On personal WhatsApp | Delete the WhatsApp account in the app, then add it as a new number |
| With another WhatsApp API provider | Select it in Meta's signup. See [Use a number from another provider](/whatsapp-api/account/existing-numbers) |

## After connecting

- **Account review.** Meta reviews new WhatsApp accounts, usually within hours. See [Account status](/whatsapp-api/account/account-status).
- **Display name review.** The name is reviewed separately. You can send before it's approved, with some limits.
- **Messaging limit.** New accounts start at 250 unique people per day. See [Messaging limits](/whatsapp-api/account/messaging-limits).
- **Wallet.** Add balance in [Billing](/whatsapp-api/dashboard/billing). Sends are refused until the wallet can cover them.

## Signup problems

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| The Meta window doesn't open | Allow pop-ups for the Karzoun site and try again |
| "The number is already registered" | It's on a WhatsApp app. Delete the account in the app, or choose **Use my WhatsApp Business app** |
| No verification code arrives | Try the voice call option. Check that the number can receive international calls |
| The portfolio or WhatsApp account can't be changed | Meta pre-selects the ones that already hold the number. That's expected |
| You don't see your portfolio | Your Facebook account isn't an admin of it. Ask an admin to connect, or to make you admin |
