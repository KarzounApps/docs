**Billing** shows your wallet, your plan and what WhatsApp messages cost.

## Wallet

WhatsApp messages are charged to your wallet. Every billable request first reserves its cost; if the wallet can't cover it, the API answers `402` and nothing is sent.

- Click **Top up wallet** and pay with a card.
- Turn on **Auto top-up** so the wallet refills when it runs low.

See [Pricing and wallet](/whatsapp-api/pricing-and-wallet) for how reservations work.

## Current plan

Your plan, its monthly price and the seats included. WhatsApp usage isn't part of the plan price; it's charged to your wallet.

## WhatsApp spend

**WhatsApp spend** lists what your numbers cost per day or week, compared with the previous period. Use it to watch campaign costs and catch unexpected spikes.

## Card charges

**Card charges** lists receipts for wallet top-ups and plan payments. WhatsApp send costs are in the spend list, not here.

## Refunds

Subscription fees can be refunded within 7 days of payment. WhatsApp usage that was already sent isn't refundable.

## Moving to the Karzoun platform

From **Compare plans** you can move to a Karzoun platform plan with a team inbox, broadcasts, automations and AI agents. Your numbers, templates, wallet and API key keep working.
