---
title: "Pricing and wallet"
description: "How WhatsApp messages are charged to your Karzoun wallet, and what a 402 means."
---

WhatsApp messages are charged per message, at WhatsApp's rate for the recipient's country and the message category. The cost is deducted from your workspace wallet.

## Reservation before each send

Every billable request reserves its expected cost from your wallet before the message is sent:

| Request | Reserves |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /messages` | Yes |
| `POST /marketing_messages` | Yes |
| `POST /calling/calls` | Yes |
| Everything else (templates, media, profile…) | No |

- The amount is based on the recipient's country (`to`, or the country of `recipient`).
- If WhatsApp rejects the message, the reservation is released at once.
- If it's accepted, the reservation stays until the final charge reaches your wallet, then the final charge replaces it.

Your **available balance** is the wallet balance minus open reservations.

## 402 Payment Required

When the available balance can't cover a send, the API answers `402` and nothing is sent:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "message": "Wallet can cover 120 cents, 300 cents are already held, this send needs 45",
    "type": "Karzoun",
    "code": 402
  }
}
```

Add funds in **Billing**, or turn on auto top-up, then retry.

<Tip>
  Sending a campaign? Check the available balance first and send in batches. Each request reserves its own cost, so a large burst can reach the limit partway through.
</Tip>

## Spend

**Billing → WhatsApp spend** shows what your numbers cost per day or week, compared with the previous period.
