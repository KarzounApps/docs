<Info>

</Info>

Topics planned for migration include:

- Getting started
- Inbox and team chat
- WhatsApp (templates, flows, pricing, CTWA)
- Broadcasts and automations
- Contacts, store, and orders
- AI, knowledge base, and settings

<Note>
  Until content is migrated, use your existing Help Center deployment or contact [developers@karzoun.chat](mailto:developers@karzoun.chat).
</Note>
