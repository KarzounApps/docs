The Help Center is for teams using Karzoun day to day — not for API integrations or partner white-label setup.

<Info>
Most Help Center articles are in **Arabic**. English pages below are short operational summaries. For the full step-by-step library, switch language to **عربي** or open [مركز المساعدة](/ar/help-center).
</Info>

<Note>
If Karzoun is down or slow for your whole team, check [Status](https://status.karzoun.chat/) before opening a support ticket. Operator steps (what to attach, where to escalate) are in Arabic: [Get support](/ar/help-center/guides/getting-started/get-support).
</Note>
