Receive and make **WhatsApp voice calls** from Karzoun — with business hours, call recording, transcripts, and optional voicemail.

This guide is for business owners and team admins. You do not need technical knowledge of Meta’s APIs.

## What you can do

| Feature              | What it means for your team                                                      |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Voice calls          | Customers can call your WhatsApp number; agents answer in the browser            |
| Call button          | Show or hide the call icon in the customer’s WhatsApp chat                       |
| Callback permission  | After a customer calls you, WhatsApp can ask if your business may call them back |
| Business hours       | Only receive calls during the hours you set                                      |
| Call recording       | Automatically save every call on the number                                      |
| Written transcripts  | Turn recorded calls into searchable text                                         |
| Voicemail (Beta)     | Let customers leave a message when nobody answers                                |
| Call link            | Share a link so customers can start a call from your website or social profiles  |
| Call history & stats | Review past calls and pickup rates                                               |

## What you need first

- A connected [WhatsApp channel](/help-center/guides/channels/doc-1889747) in Karzoun
- Calling enabled for your number in **WhatsApp Manager** (Meta), when Meta requires it
- A modern browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended) with **microphone** permission
- A stable internet connection (wired is best for long calls)

<Info>
**Messaging limit:** Meta usually requires a WhatsApp messaging limit of **2,000 or higher** before calling can be enabled on a business number.
</Info>

### Country limits for outbound calls

Customers can **call you** from most countries. Your business **calling customers** may be restricted in some regions (for example United States, Canada, Egypt, Vietnam, and Nigeria). If outbound calling is blocked for your number, inbound calling and messaging still work.

## Turn on calling in Karzoun

1. Go to **Channels → WhatsApp** and open your connected number
2. Open the **Calling** tab
3. Turn on **Allow WhatsApp calls**
4. Optionally turn on **Show the call button** so customers see a call icon in WhatsApp
5. Optionally turn on **Ask permission to call customers back**

Changes can take time to appear for every WhatsApp user. Opening your business chat info page in WhatsApp often refreshes the call button sooner.

---

## Business hours

Use business hours when you only want to receive calls while your team is available.

1. In the **Calling** tab, turn on **Only receive calls during set hours**
2. Choose your **business time zone**
3. Add weekly periods (up to two periods per day)
4. Add **special dates** for holidays or different hours on a specific day
5. Save

Calls outside these hours are blocked. Customers can still send WhatsApp messages.

<Warning>
If you enable **voicemail**, Meta recommends turning **business hours** off for calling — customers cannot place calls outside hours, so they would never reach voicemail.
</Warning>

## Answer and place calls

### Incoming calls

- An incoming call shows a ringing notification in Karzoun
- Accept (green) or decline (red)
- If nobody answers in time, the call is marked missed (and may go to voicemail if you enabled it)

### Outgoing calls

1. Open the customer’s WhatsApp conversation
2. Tap the **phone** icon in the conversation header
3. Speak with the customer in the browser softphone

### During a call

- Mute / unmute the microphone
- End the call with the red button
- If the connection drops briefly, Karzoun tries to reconnect automatically

### Call button message

From the composer tools, you can send an interactive **Call** button so the customer starts the call from their side. Set the button text and how long the button stays valid.

## Record calls and create written conversations

Recording is configured **per WhatsApp number**, not per single call.

1. Open **Calling → Record and summarize calls**
2. Turn on **Automatically record calls**
3. Write a short sentence for **What customers should hear** (spoken consent notice)
4. Choose the **language of the spoken notice**
5. Optionally turn on **Create a written conversation** and choose the language spoken during calls
6. Save

<Info>
Customers hear a short spoken notice at the start of the call when recording (and/or transcription) is enabled. Keep the purpose text clear and honest.
</Info>

After the call ends:

- The **recording** appears in call history and the conversation timeline
- The **transcript** (if enabled) appears as readable text agents and AI tools can use

## Voicemail (Beta)

Voicemail lets WhatsApp answer when your team declines a call or does not pick up in time, play your greeting, and deliver the customer’s message as an audio note in the Inbox.

<Warning>
**Beta:** Meta is still rolling out voicemail for WhatsApp Business accounts. Upload or save may fail until Meta enables the feature for your account. Recording and other calling features are unaffected.
</Warning>

### How to set it up

1. Turn on **Let customers leave a voice message**
2. Choose when voicemail should answer:
   - When the team declines the call
   - When nobody answers
3. Set how many **seconds** the phone rings before voicemail answers (0–30)
4. Upload a short **greeting** (OGG/Opus audio, under 60 seconds)
5. Save when the greeting shows as ready

Voicemail messages arrive in the conversation as audio. They are linked to the original call.

## Shareable call link

In the **Calling** tab, copy the **shareable call link** and place it on your website, email signature, or social profiles. Opening the link takes the customer into a WhatsApp call with your business.

---

## Call history and analytics

- **Call history** lists inbound and outbound calls with status and duration
- **Analytics** shows totals, completion rate, missed rate, and average duration

Use these to spot pickup problems early — Meta may hide the call button if too many calls go unanswered.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem                     | What to try                                                                    |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| No call notification        | Check your internet connection and that calling is enabled on the channel      |
| Microphone blocked          | Allow the microphone for Karzoun in browser settings                           |
| No microphone found         | Unplug/replug the headset; close other apps using the mic                      |
| Call drops often            | Prefer a wired connection; avoid unstable Wi‑Fi                                |
| Phone icon missing in chat  | Enable calling and the call button in the Calling tab                          |
| Outbound call not available | Your number may be in a restricted country for business-initiated calls        |
| Cannot hear the other party | Check speaker volume; try a headset                                            |
| Voicemail upload fails      | The feature may still be Beta for your Meta account — wait for Meta enablement |
| Recording not available     | Confirm recording is saved on the Calling tab and that calling is enabled      |

## Privacy

- Call audio travels between the agent’s browser and Meta over encrypted WebRTC
- Karzoun stores call **metadata** (who, when, duration, status) and, when enabled, copies of **recordings** and **transcripts** in your workspace storage
- Share recordings only with people who need them for quality, training, or compliance
