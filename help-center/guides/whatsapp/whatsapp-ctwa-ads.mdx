When someone sees your ad on Facebook or Instagram and taps **Message**, WhatsApp opens to your business. That moment is valuable — and easy to waste.

With Karzoun you can:

1. **Design the first screen** they see (your greeting + how they reply)
2. **Show your team which ad** they came from (image, headline, text)
3. **Start the right workflow** automatically (sales, support, shopping)

This guide explains **why it matters**, **how to set it up**, and **how to get more value** from every ad click — without needing a technical meeting.

---

## Why this matters

A normal WhatsApp ad often drops the customer into an empty chat. They hesitate, type something vague, or leave.

A clear first screen does three jobs at once:

| Goal             | What you design                         | Business result                    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Warm welcome     | Short greeting from your brand          | Feels intentional, not random      |
| Easy first reply | Ready-to-send text **or** quick buttons | More people reply (less friction)  |
| Smart routing    | Buttons like “Pricing” / “Support”      | Right team, faster, fewer handoffs |

**Extra win:** your agents see the ad creative in the Inbox, so they never ask “which offer did you click?”

---

## The customer journey (simple picture)

1. Customer taps **Message** on your ad
2. WhatsApp opens
3. They see **your greeting**, plus either:
   - a **ready-to-send message** already typed for them, **or**
   - up to **3 quick reply buttons**
4. They send / tap
5. In Karzoun Inbox, the conversation opens with an **ad card** (what they saw)
6. Optional: an **Automation** assigns, tags, or replies based on the ad or the button they chose

<Info>
Facebook allows **either** a ready-to-send message **or** quick reply buttons on the same first message — not both. Choose the style that fits the campaign.
</Info>

## What you need first

- A connected [WhatsApp channel](/help-center/guides/channels/doc-1889747) in Karzoun
- Access to **Facebook Ads Manager** (Meta)
- A WhatsApp ad that opens a chat with your business number

<Info>
Designing the **first message** in Karzoun needs Facebook to enable this for your WhatsApp account. If **Ads Manager → First message after the ad** shows unavailable, you can still use the Inbox ad card and Automations from ad clicks — only creating first messages inside Karzoun is blocked until Facebook enables it. Contact support if you believe it should already be on.
</Info>

## Part 1 — Design the first message (in Karzoun)

Open **Channels → WhatsApp → Ads Manager**.

The section **First message after the ad** is where you create what customers see.

### Create one

1. Click **Create message**, or **Use a ready template**
2. Give it a clear **Name** (example: “Spring sale — sales leads”)
3. Write **Your greeting** — what your business says first
4. Choose **how the customer replies** (one tab only):
   - **Ready-to-send message** — text already typed; they tap Send
   - **Quick reply buttons** — up to 3 short buttons
5. Save — Karzoun syncs it to Facebook and shows an **ID for ads**

### Ready templates (good starting points)

| Template     | Best for                         | Style                 |
| ------------ | -------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Sales leads  | Offers, demos, pricing questions | Quick buttons         |
| Support help | Billing / technical / other      | Quick buttons         |
| Shopping     | Product interest                 | Ready-to-send message |

### Writing tips that raise reply rates

- Make the greeting **campaign-specific** (“Thanks for checking our spring offer”) not generic (“Hello”)
- Keep buttons **short and different** (“Pricing”, “Book a demo”, “Talk to us”) so people pick one clearly
- Prefer **one first message per campaign type** (sales vs support vs shopping) instead of one message for everything
- Ready-to-send text should sound like something a real customer would type

### Copy the ID

In the list, click the **ID for ads** (or **Copy ID for ads**). You will use it when setting up the ad.

<Warning>
If a first message shows **Connected to an ad**, Facebook locks it. Disconnect it from the ad in Ads Manager before you edit or delete it in Karzoun.
</Warning>

## Part 2 — Connect it to your ad (Facebook Ads Manager)

1. Open **Facebook Ads Manager** and edit (or create) your WhatsApp message ad
2. In **Message template**, choose **Partner app**
3. Select your messaging partner app (Karzoun / as shown by Facebook)
4. Under the message list, pick the **first message** you created in Karzoun
5. Preview and publish

Then run a real test: click the ad yourself, send a message, and confirm it arrives in Karzoun.

---

## Part 3 — See the ad in the Inbox

When a customer messages you from an ad, the **first message** in the thread shows an **ad card**:

- Ad image (or video thumbnail)
- Headline and main text they saw
- Optional greeting from the first message
- Name of the first message (when Karzoun can match it)
- **View ad** link

Agents get context instantly — no awkward “which campaign?” questions.

### Tips for your team

- Scroll to the **top** of the thread if you do not see the card
- Conversations started before this feature may not have a full card
- If the image is missing, headline and text still help when Facebook sent them

## Part 4 — Get more juice with Automations

Every ad click can start a WhatsApp event:

- **Trigger:** WhatsApp Events → **Ad Referral**

Useful ideas (publish the automation after you test):

### Route by button

1. Trigger: Ad Referral
2. If the customer’s first text is `Pricing` (exact button title)
3. Assign to Sales / send a catalog / add tag `ad-pricing`

### Tag every ad lead

1. Trigger: Ad Referral
2. Add tag `from-whatsapp-ad` and put the conversation in your Leads queue

### Different first messages → different teams

1. Trigger: Ad Referral
2. If the first-message name is `Shopping`
3. Assign to Commerce

<Info>
Button routing works best when the customer’s first text **exactly matches** the button title. Keep titles short and consistent.
</Info>

See also: [Automations](/help-center/guides/automations/basics/doc-1890493).

## Playbooks by business goal

### Sales / lead generation

- Greeting: thank them for the offer they saw
- Buttons: Pricing · Book a demo · Talk to us
- Automation: Pricing → Sales; Talk to us → human queue

### Support campaigns

- Greeting: “Tell us what you need help with”
- Buttons: Billing · Technical issue · Something else
- Automation: each button → the right support team or FAQ reply

### Shopping / catalog

- Greeting: invite them to browse
- Ready-to-send: “Hi! I want to see your products.”
- Automation: open catalog or assign to Commerce

### Multiple offers at once

Create a **separate first message per offer**. Agents and reports stay clearer, and Ads Manager stays easier to manage.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see                      | What to check                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No ad card in Inbox               | The chat must start from an ad click (not a normal WhatsApp chat). Open the first message.      |
| Image missing, text still there   | Image download may have failed once; headline/caption still show when Facebook sent them.       |
| First message section unavailable | Facebook has not enabled it for this WhatsApp account yet. Contact Karzoun support.             |
| Cannot edit or delete             | Status is **Connected to an ad**. Disconnect it in Ads Manager first.                           |
| Message missing in Ads Manager    | Create/refresh in Karzoun, wait a moment, refresh Ads Manager, confirm Partner app is selected. |
| Automation did not run            | Published? Trigger = WhatsApp Events → Ad Referral? Correct WhatsApp channel?                   |
| Button not detected               | First customer text must **exactly match** the button title.                                    |
